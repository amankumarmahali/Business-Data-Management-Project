## 💡 Recommendations Summary

### 📦 Inventory Management

- ✅ For the academic season, the bookstore should procure 100% of the projected demand
by March 25th, and maintain a Safety Stock of 10 days peak volume throughout April.

- ✅ For the non academic season, the bookstore should focus on the other categories like
Paper Goods, Stationary, etc.

- 🧊 For the top 5 fast-Selling items the bookstore should pay more attention on the number of
stocks left on the bookstore and once the items quantity dips below the reorder point,
they should place an order for the same.

- 📈 For the faster delivery of the goods, the communication should be strong with the
suppliers 


### 📉 Lack of Customer Loyalty

- 🧾 Enforce a mandatory 50% advance payment for any item not currently in stock, for the
customers whose RFM score is not good.

- 🧾 Zero-Deposit Privilege to customers whose RFM score is good and has a proven track
record.
